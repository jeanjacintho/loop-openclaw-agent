# LP-10 — Digest and attention budget

## What changed

- `skills/loop/scripts/digest.ts`:
  - `pick`: at most 5 items, in this order: critical (due today **and** owed
    by the owner to an investor or customer, or blocking another commitment)
    → what the owner owes, overdue → what the owner is owed, overdue
    (blockers first) → "looks done?" (from LP-9) → at most 2 candidates never
    asked → "are these two the same person?" (LP-8). Snoozed commitments stay
    out until their snooze ends. No deadline → the type's default, marked
    "(sem prazo dito)" / "(no deadline given)".
  - Silence: nothing to show → `send: false`. The same items as the last
    digest, less than 3 days later → `send: false` too (no daily nagging
    about what the owner already saw); anything new, or 3 days, sends again.
  - Text in the owner's language (`pt` / `en` from `config.language`),
    numbered, with a reply hint, exactly the plan's shape:
    `🔁 Loop — 3 coisas hoje` / `1. Michael espera: o deck — venceu ontem.` /
    `Responda com o número: "1 feito", "2 cobra", …`.
  - Mac off: a source that is failing, or not read for 2 hours, adds
    "Não li mensagens novas desde <quando> (o Mac pode estar desligado)." The
    digest itself comes from SQLite and does not need the Mac (Review Focus 6).
  - Two phases: `pick` remembers the pick, `sent` makes its numbers the ones
    the owner's replies refer to (`item --n N`) and marks candidates and
    person questions as asked, so they are asked once.
  - `alerts`: real time only for critical items, once per commitment per day,
    never between 21:00 and 08:00 in the owner's zone, never for what the
    backfill found (LP-13 flags it).
  - `why --n N`: the commitment and its evidence (D5).
- `skills/loop-digest/SKILL.md`: retention, pick, send as printed, `sent`.
- The poll sends critical alerts at its end; the owner's "why?" uses `why`.
