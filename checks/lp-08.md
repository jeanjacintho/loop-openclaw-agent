# LP-8 — People and deduplication

## What changed

- `skills/loop/scripts/people.ts`:
  - Contacts: one fixed argv (`CONTACTS_ARGV`: `sqlite3 -readonly` over every
    AddressBook source; names, orgs, emails, phones; nothing else), at most
    once a day, cached in `contacts.json`, so the unattended poll never asks
    Latch per person (D8). This replaces the unverified `contacts` CLI of S5.
  - `enrich(person)`: adds every handle the owner's contacts list for that
    person, their contact name when the message gave none, and the role their
    email domain carries (`config.domainRoles`). Never a handle from text.
  - `role`, `questions`, `answer --same|--different`, `show`.
- `ledger.ts`:
  - `personByHandle` matches phones on their last digits (Meetly's `sameHandle`).
  - A new person whose name equals a known person's, with no shared handle,
    becomes a `person_questions` row (migration 003) for the digest to ask
    once. Never merged by name alone; `answer --same` moves handles,
    commitments and evidence to one person.
  - Repeats (Review Focus 2 / F6): a new commitment between the same debtor
    and creditor, same direction and `object_kind`, sharing a content word
    (delivery verbs and stop words removed, pt/en), live and detected in the
    last 7 days → one more `update` evidence on the existing commitment
    (`created: false, merged: true`).
- `detect.ts` enriches the other side before recording.
- The poll refreshes contacts first; setup's "After setup" takes roles.

## Limits worth a look

- Cross-channel merge needs the owner's contacts to hold both the email and
  the phone. Without that, "Michael (email)" and "Michael (phone)" are two
  people, and the digest asks whether they are the same.
- "Sharing a content word" is deliberately loose within one pair of people and
  one week ("send the deck" / "mando o deck" merge); two different files for
  the same person in one week that share a word would merge too. The merged
  evidence stays visible in `ledger.ts get`.
