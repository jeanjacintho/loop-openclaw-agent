# LP-12 — The owner's feedback and calibration

## What changed

- `skills/loop/scripts/feedback.ts`: one owner event per command: `done`,
  `not` (rejected, with the commitment's band and features in the event),
  `yes` (confirmed), `reopen` ("not yet"), `postpone` / `snooze` (the owner's
  words, read from **now** in their zone), `ignore --by kind|person`, and
  `note` ("anota: prometi X pro Y até Z": an open commitment with the owner's
  message as `plow:<chat>:<ms>` evidence; the quote must be in it).
- `migrations/004_ignore.sql` + `detect.ts`: a detection matching an ignore
  rule (same object kind and type, or the same person) is counted as dropped
  and never recorded.
- `ledger.ts`:
  - `stats` adds owner-verified precision by band, by source, and by both.
    A detection's verdict is the owner's first act on it: confirming,
    closing, moving, snoozing or chasing it = right; "not a commitment" =
    wrong. Automatic closes are not verdicts.
  - `calibrate`: over the last 20 verdicts on `open` detections, below 0.8 →
    the open cut rises one step (`calibration.open_raise`, at most +2, used by
    `detect.ts`), reported once (`raised: true`); only verdicts after a raise
    count toward the next one.
- `skills/loop-owner/SKILL.md`: the table of replies and corrections, the
  questions ("o que eu devo?", "o que me devem?", "o que tenho com o
  Michael?"), notes, and the one-line notice when the cut rises.
