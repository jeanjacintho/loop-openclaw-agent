# LP-13 — Onboarding: the first ten minutes

## What changed

- `skills/loop/scripts/backfill.ts`:
  - `run`: with the owner present, reads 14 days once (mail sent and inbox:
    `plow-gog gmail search "in:<box> newer_than:14d" --max 150 …`; iMessage:
    `plow-messages search --limit 1000 --order desc`, cut to 14 days). The
    newest 150 messages the owner sent go through the same prefilter as the
    poll; every message in the window is kept as evidence, so what was
    already delivered gets closed instead of reported as open. Candidates are
    flagged `backfill`.
  - `summary`: one message, in the owner's language: "Achei N coisas que você
    prometeu e M que te devem nas últimas 2 semanas. K já passaram do prazo.
    J já foram entregues." + up to 3 past-due items with their quote + up to
    5 "not sure" candidates, numbered for "4 sim / 5 não". Nothing open →
    one line saying so.
  - `done`: the look-back's messages are deleted from the state volume; the
    candidates it showed count as asked; its numbers become what "1 feito"
    refers to, without suppressing the first digest.
- Found by the look-back ⇒ `detected.payload.backfill` ⇒ never a real-time
  alert, even when critical; it goes into the first digest.
- Deadlines come from when each message was sent (LP-6), so an old "amanhã"
  is overdue.
- `loop-setup`: after `--done`, "can I look at the last two weeks now?"; on
  yes, **Look back** (run → Extract → Judge → summary → done), in the same turn.
