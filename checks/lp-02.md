# LP-2 — Ledger: data model and single writer

## What changed

- `skills/loop/scripts/migrations/001_init.sql`: `people`, `handles`,
  `commitments`, `evidence`, `events`, `edges`.
- `skills/loop/scripts/db.ts`: SQLite through `node:sqlite` (AHA pattern): WAL,
  numbered migrations under one write lock, `BEGIN IMMEDIATE` per write,
  re-entrant `tx`, `secure_delete` so cleared quotes are really gone.
- `skills/loop/scripts/ledger.ts`: the single writer and its CLI (`add`,
  `event`, `evidence`, `get`, `list`, `find`, `due`, `stats`, `retain`).
  State changes only by events; `project(events)` derives status, deadline,
  snooze and who closed it, and the row is rewritten from that projection.
- `skills/loop/scripts/confidence.ts`: `band(features, raise)` →
  `open | candidate | drop`, one weight table, one pair of cuts.
- `skills/loop/scripts/handles.ts`: Meetly's `normalizeHandle`/`sameHandle`,
  plus typed handles (`email`, `phone`, `plow:<uid>`).
- `skills/loop/SKILL.md`, "How Loop works" in the prompt, `tests/skills.test.ts`
  (every script a skill names exists).

## Choices worth a look

- The owner is a row in `people` (`is_owner = 1`), so debtor and creditor are
  always people. `i_owe` requires debtor `"owner"`, `they_owe` requires
  creditor `"owner"`.
- The dedupe key is `sha256(source | item | normalized what)` of the origin
  evidence, `UNIQUE`. A repeated `add` returns `created: false` and creates no
  person.
- Snooze keeps the deadline; `due` shows a snoozed commitment again once its
  snooze has ended.
- Retention (F15): `retain` clears the quote (not the item) of evidence on
  commitments closed more than 90 days ago.
- Confidence: a pleasantry without a concrete object is always `drop`; a
  conditional or an unclear counterparty is at most `candidate` (F21). The
  calibration offset only raises the open cut, capped at +2.
