# LP-6 — Deadlines

## What changed

`skills/loop/scripts/deadline.ts`: `resolveDeadline({text, sentAt, tz, locale})`
→ `{kind:"date", at, text, certainty}` | `{kind:"event", event, text}` |
`{kind:"none", text}`, plus `defaultDeadline(type, sentAt, tz)` for the digest.

- Always relative to `sentAt`, in the owner's zone (Review Focus 4): a
  "tomorrow" found by the two-week backfill is two weeks overdue, and 23:30 on
  Wednesday in São Paulo is still Wednesday even though it is Thursday in UTC.
- A day without a time is 18:00 that day; "às 15h" / "at 3pm" sets the time.
- Weekdays: the next one; the same day only when the message was sent before
  17:00 that day ("sexta" sent on Friday evening is next Friday).
  "next Tuesday" / "terça que vem" is the coming Tuesday, never today, `soft`.
- "semana que vem" / "next week" → Monday of next week, `soft`. "fim do mês",
  "fim da semana" → that day. "até dia 10" / "by the 10th" → this month if
  still ahead, else next month. `10/10` is day/month except for `en`/`en-US`.
- "depois do board" / "after the meeting with Sarah" → `kind: "event"`
  (matched to the calendar in LP-17). "depois do almoço" is today.
- No deadline → `kind: "none"`. `defaultDeadline` gives the digest a day to
  ask about: a promise 3 working days after it was sent, anything asked of
  someone else 5, `certainty: "inferred"`.
